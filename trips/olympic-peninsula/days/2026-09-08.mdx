We woke up with our eyes set on the big hike of our trip: Heather Park via Lake Angeles Trail. Luckily the trailhead was only about a 15 minute drive from our new motel. 

The hike started with a slow ascent in a heavily wooded area of old growth. 

![img-5251-2](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5251-2.jpg)

![img-5250-2](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5250-2.jpg)

Over the next few hours we made progress up the hill. 

After about an hour there was a big of a code brown by yours truly on the side of the trail. Anyway moving past that, we eventually made it up onto the beginning of a ridge. 

![img-5256-2](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5256-2.jpg)

And got our first good view of Lake Angeles

![img-5269-2](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5269-2.jpg)

The climb continued and we began to get some amazing views. Seeing Port Angeles, Vancover Island, and Mount Baker.


![img-5274-2](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5274-2.jpg)



![img-5289-1](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5289-1.jpg)
