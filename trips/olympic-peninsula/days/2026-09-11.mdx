The next day we attended the annual Port Townsend Wooden Boat Festival. There was lots of different booths and boats, including a woodworking demonstration. We got sucked into a strange and extremely boring talk about geometric construction using a compass and straight edge, and then later a talk about harbor porpoises. Later we built our own sailboat at the kids corner.

After we left, we went home to eat dinner and then felt the desire to go for a walk. Being at an airbnb in the middle of a forest, it was extremely dark out and we decided to drive back to Port Townsend to go for our walk. During the drive we were assaulted by one of the most disgusting smells of our entire lives, which followed us for around 10 minutes of the drive. After we got to the city, thankfully the smell subsided and we began walking around. Although it was a small city and it was pretty late, we saw several people riding bicycles and walking around. We eventually walked all the way to the entrance to the boat festival and saw that there was nothing stopping us from entering. As we walked in, we heard music that sounded much too good to be live. But, in the distance we saw a stage with a decent crowd of people. Unexpectedly we stumbled onto a concert that was still going on from the festival!

There was a strange cohort of older white people kind of half dancing and a few food trucks that were still open. After we stayed until they finished, we walked back, took the car back and saw a factory spewing out some kind of gray fog. On google maps the factory had a 1 star review talking about the sulfur dioxide that it billows out. 

We made it back to our airbnb and went to bed in preparation for our last day.
