One of them fell over when she saw me approaching, and then prevented me from passing her as I continued, which I thought was kind of cute but annoying and strange. I kind of knew Julia would be unable to pass them so eventually I went to the back of their group (I felt like they smugly looked at me as they passed me so I could get to Julia like “oh we’re going too fast for you?”) and asked if she could please pass. They got mad and I yelled at them when the father(?) said “well thats the going rate.” I asked him “do you know how hiking works?” there was some murmur from the others of “you could have asked politely.” I replied “shes been trying to pass you guys for 10 minutes.” After we passed the parents it took us about 10 more minutes to pass the kids that were with them who did not want us to pass them. They were running as fast as they could to prevent us from passing them, before running out of breath so we would almost catch up to them. EVentually we passed them and got down about 20m minutes before the did. Strange.
