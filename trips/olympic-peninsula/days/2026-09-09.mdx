We decided to go stand up paddleboarding finally at Lake Crescent. I had never done it in my life and was very shaky at the beginning, but I eventually got the hang of it. 

The water was glassy at first and we traversed the lake in about 20 minutes, before spending the next hour or so floating around exploring.

![img-5303-2](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5303-2.jpg)

A wind started to pick up and we then spent a good 30 minutes pushing against the wind to make our slow progress back. 

![img-5320-1](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5320-1.jpg)

After we were finished, we went swimming off the dock with a rather strange 20 something oversharer would did cool flips and stuff into the water. 


![img-5323-1](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5323-1.jpg)

Afterwards we went to Grandma's house cafe for dinner which had decent burgers and a farm in the back with emus.


![img-5327-1](/trip-media/olympic-peninsula/photos/img-5327-1.jpg)
